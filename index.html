About Me

Han Li is a Master's student in Computer Science at the University of Science and Technology of China (USTC), supervised by Prof. Guangzhong Sun and Dr. Jingwei Sun. He received his B.S. in Computer Science and Technology as an Outstanding Graduate from Jilin University (JLU). Han has previously interned at Ctrip Group as a Software Engineer and later at MetaX through the AI Computing and General Computing Library Talent Program.

His current research interests focus on Efficient AI, including inference speed optimization for Mixture of Experts (MoE), kernel optimization, model quantization, and efficient serving systems. Driven by an insatiable curiosity, he constantly seeks to expand his knowledge and embrace new challenges.
