About Me

Han Li is pursuing his Master degree in Computer Science at the University of Science and Technology of China (USTC), supervised by Prof. Guangzhong Sun and Dr. Jingwei Sun. He received his B.S. in Computer Science and Technology as an Outstanding Graduate from Jilin University (JLU). Han has previously interned at Ctrip Group as a Software Engineer and later at MetaX through the AI Computing and General Computing Library Talent Program.

His current research interests focus on MLSYS, including optimizing inference speed for Mixture of Experts (MoE), operator optimization, model quantization, and efficient serving systems. He has published as first author or co-author in AAAI (CCF A), TSE (CCF A), and TOSEM (CCF A). Driven by an insatiable curiosity, he constantly seeks to expand his knowledge and embrace new challenges.
